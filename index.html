A focused day for language research

The second edition of the Copenhagen NLP Symposium takes place on 22 October 2026 as a pre-EMNLP event. It brings together researchers, students, and industry practitioners working on natural language processing, with international keynotes, a poster session, and dedicated time to discuss work across the field.

Present your work

We invite researchers to present posters on any topic related to Natural Language Processing. Posters may showcase recently published work (e.g., at conferences or in journals) or ongoing research. The registration form contains a dedicated section to express an interest in presenting a poster. Please include the poster title, an abstract, and—if applicable—details about the publication venue.

Topics include but are not limited to

  • Safety and Alignment in LLMs
  • AI/LLM Agents
  • Human–AI Interaction and Cooperation
  • Retrieval-Augmented Language Models
  • Mathematical, Symbolic, and Logical Reasoning
  • Computational Social Science and NLP for Social Good
  • Interpretability, Model Editing, Transparency, and Explainability
  • Dialogue, Pragmatics, and Reasoning
  • Ethics, Bias, and Fairness
  • Machine Translation and Multilinguality
  • Speech Processing and Spoken Language Understanding
  • Resources, Evaluation, Semantics, Syntax, and NLP Applications
